# Insights — Layer 1 Style Guide (EN + FA)

Used by `INSIGHTS-L1-PLAIN-LANGUAGE-SPEC.md`. If this guide conflicts with wording already approved and live on the site (status chips, summary labels, Persian terminology), the approved site wording wins.

## 1. The reader

An adult, smart, busy, with NO finance education. Never heard of "yield", "breakeven", "spread" or "PMI". Wants to understand what is going on and get leads for saving and investing. Reads on a phone. In Farsi, reads the way Iranian economic news sites write for ordinary people.

## 2. Voice

Clear, concise, consistent, useful, human (in that order of importance). A calm, knowledgeable friend explaining over tea: never a textbook, never a broker, never a headline writer. Describe what usually happens; never tell the reader what to do.

## 3. Hard rules — English

1. Replace jargon with a plain word. If a term truly cannot be avoided, explain it in the same sentence or list it as a glossary term.
2. Short sentences. Average ≤ 20 words, never over 28. One idea per sentence.
3. Everyday consequences first: prices, petrol, groceries, loans, savings, rent, jobs, travel.
4. Hedge honestly: usually, often, tends to, can. Never always, will, guaranteed.
5. Describe, don't advise: no buy, sell, invest, should, must, "good time to".
6. State-independent: write "when oil gets more expensive…", never "oil is high".
7. No numbers in static copy (except marked glossary examples).
8. Active voice, present tense, no filler ("it is important to note that").
9. One word per concept across the whole page. Pick it once, reuse it.

## 4. Hard rules — Farsi (write natively, never translate word-for-word)

1. Sound like a clear Iranian finance explainer for ordinary readers: simple, spoken-formal register, short sentences.
2. Use the terms Iranians already know from Iranian economic and stock sites (e.g. تورم، نرخ بهره، اوراق قرضه، شاخص بورس، اونس طلا، نفت برنت). Match wording already used on the site.
3. Persian digits only (۰–۹). Persian letters ی و ک (never Arabic ي ك). Correct half-space (نیم‌فاصله) in words like «می‌شود»، «بالا می‌رود».
4. No Latin letters inside Farsi sentences except the allow-list: VIX, S&P, ASX, ECB, RBA, USD, AUD, EUR, JPY, CNY, BTC, PMI. Prefer the Persian phrase and put the symbol in parentheses only if the site already does.
5. Hedge: «معمولاً»، «اغلب»، «ممکن است»، «تمایل دارد». Never «حتماً»، «قطعاً»، «تضمین».
6. Never address the reader with commands. Describe, don't instruct.
7. Direction wording must be natural RTL: avoid arrows or symbols inside sentences.
8. The Farsi must carry the same meaning as the English, but it need not mirror its sentence structure.

## 5. Banned words (the validator enforces these; extend the lists)

EN advice/certainty: buy, sell, invest now, should, must, guaranteed, certainly, definitely, will crash, will rise, will fall, sure thing, "good time to", "bad time to", get rich, risk-free.

FA advice/certainty: بخرید، بفروشید، سرمایه‌گذاری کنید، حتماً، قطعاً، تضمین، بدون ریسک، زمان خوب برای، زمان بد برای.

## 6. Jargon seed list (each must be replaced or be a glossary term)

yield, real yield, breakeven, inflation expectations, spread, basis points, volatility, VIX, PMI, hawkish, dovish, tightening, easing, quantitative easing, risk-on, risk-off, safe haven, correction, bear market, bull market, drawdown, momentum, overbought, oversold, benchmark, commodity, futures, curve inversion, yield curve, CPI, GDP, central bank, policy rate, ratio, dollar index, equities, bond, liquidity, headline vs core.

## 7. Glossary (required)

Every term above that survives in the copy gets an entry in both languages: `{ term, plain (≤ 2 sentences), example? }`. Examples may contain numbers but must be marked as examples. Seed entries (refine, extend, keep tone):

- inflation — EN: "How fast prices are rising. If inflation is 3%, something that cost $100 last year costs about $103 now." FA: «سرعت گران شدن قیمت‌ها. اگر تورم ۳ درصد باشد، چیزی که پارسال ۱۰۰ دلار بود، حالا حدود ۱۰۳ دلار است.»
- bond — EN: "A loan you give to a government or a company. They pay you interest and give the money back later." FA: «وامی که به یک دولت یا شرکت می‌دهید. آن‌ها به شما سود می‌دهند و بعداً پول را برمی‌گردانند.» (use the site's existing term, e.g. اوراق قرضه)
- yield — EN: "The yearly return you get from lending money through a bond." FA: «بازدهی سالانه‌ای که از وام دادن از طریق اوراق قرضه به دست می‌آورید.»
- interest rate — EN: "The price of borrowing money. Higher rates make loans and mortgages cost more and make saving pay more." FA: «قیمت قرض گرفتن پول. نرخ بالاتر یعنی وام گران‌تر و سود پس‌انداز بیشتر.»
- central bank — EN: "The institution that controls a country's interest rates and money supply, like the Reserve Bank of Australia or the US Fed." FA: «نهادی که نرخ بهره و حجم پول یک کشور را کنترل می‌کند، مثل بانک مرکزی استرالیا یا فدرال رزرو آمریکا.»
- volatility — EN: "How wildly prices jump around. High volatility means big, fast swings." FA: «میزان نوسان شدید قیمت‌ها. نوسان بالا یعنی بالا و پایین رفتن‌های بزرگ و سریع.»
- safe haven — EN: "Something people tend to move money into when they are worried, like gold." FA: «چیزی که مردم هنگام نگرانی پولشان را به آن منتقل می‌کنند، مثل طلا.»
- commodity — EN: "A raw material that is bought and sold in bulk, like oil, gold or wheat." FA: «ماده خامی که به‌صورت عمده خرید و فروش می‌شود، مثل نفت، طلا یا گندم.»
- recession — EN: "A period when a country's economy shrinks and more people lose jobs." FA: «دوره‌ای که اقتصاد یک کشور کوچک می‌شود و افراد بیشتری شغلشان را از دست می‌دهند.»
- stock index — EN: "One number that tracks how a group of big companies' shares is doing overall." FA: «یک عدد که وضعیت کلی سهام گروهی از شرکت‌های بزرگ را نشان می‌دهد.»

## 8. Worked examples

Bad (jargon, bare number, no meaning):
"US 10-year real yield 2.95% — elevated vs. 10Y history; hawkish repricing risk."

Good:
- plainName EN: "Safe lending rate after inflation" / FA: «سود وام‌دادن امن، پس از کسر تورم»
- oneLine EN: "What lending to the US government pays after inflation. When it rises, gold tends to lose appeal." FA: «سودی که وام دادن به دولت آمریکا بعد از کسر تورم دارد. وقتی بالا می‌رود، معمولاً طلا جذابیت کمتری پیدا می‌کند.»
- ifRising EN: "Safe savings pay more, so assets that pay no interest, like gold, often get less attractive." FA: «سود پس‌انداز امن بیشتر می‌شود و دارایی‌های بدون سود، مثل طلا، اغلب جذابیت کمتری پیدا می‌کنند.»

Bad: "Oil is surging, inflationary pressure building."
Good oneLine EN: "When oil gets more expensive, petrol and many everyday prices tend to rise too." FA: «وقتی نفت گران می‌شود، معمولاً بنزین و بسیاری از قیمت‌های روزمره هم بالا می‌روند.»

Good connection (strength `usually`): EN: "A stronger US dollar usually makes gold cheaper for people holding other currencies, which often weighs on its price." FA: «قوی‌تر شدن دلار آمریکا معمولاً طلا را برای دارندگان ارزهای دیگر ارزان‌تر می‌کند و اغلب بر قیمت آن فشار می‌آورد.»

## 9. Self-test before saving each indicator

1. Cover the official name. Does `oneLine` alone tell a normal person something useful?
2. Could the owner explain it to a friend in one breath?
3. Is every sentence true whatever today's numbers are?
4. Any word a non-economist would pause on? Replace it or add it to the glossary.
5. Does the Farsi say the same thing, in natural Farsi?
6. Any advice, prediction or certainty word? Remove it.
