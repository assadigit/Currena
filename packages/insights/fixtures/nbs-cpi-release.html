In September 2026, China’s Consumer Price Index (CPI) increased by 0.9% year on year. Specifically, the price index in urban areas increased by 0.9% while that in rural areas increased by 0.8%; the price index for food decreased by 1.7% while that for non-food increased by 1.5%; the price index for consumer goods increased by 1.0% while that for services increased by 0.8%. From January to September, on average, China’s CPI increased by 0.9% year on year.

 

In September, China’s CPI increased by 0.2% month on month. Specifically, the price index in urban areas increased by 0.2% while that in rural areas increased by 0.4%; the price index for food increased by 0.4% while that for non-food increased by 0.2%; the price index for consumer goods increased by 0.5% while that for services decreased by 0.2%.

 

 

I. Year-on-Year Changes in Price Indexes for Various Goods and Services

 

In September, the price index for food, tobacco, liquor and dining out decreased by 0.9% year on year, bringing the CPI down by about 0.26 percentage points. Among food, the price index for fresh vegetables decreased by 8.1%, bringing the CPI down by about 0.14 percentage points; that for meat of livestock decreased by 3.7%, bringing the CPI down by about 0.15 percentage points, of which the price index for pork decreased by 10.0%; that for fresh fruits decreased by 1.5%, bringing the CPI down by about 0.03 percentage points; that for eggs increased by 14.3%, pushing the CPI up by about 0.07 percentage points.

 

Among the price indexes for the other seven major categories, six increased, and one decreased year on year. Specifically, the price indexes for miscellaneous goods and services, transportation and telecommunication, and health care and medical services increased by 5.9%, 4.3% and 2.7%, respectively; the price indexes for education, culture and recreation, clothing and household facilities, articles and services increased by 1.4%, 1.2% and 0.2%, respectively; and the price index for residence decreased by 0.3%.

 

 

II. Month-on-Month Changes in Price Indexes for Various Goods and Services

 

In September, the price index for food, tobacco, liquor and dining out increased by 0.2% month on month, pushing the CPI up by about 0.06 percentage points. Among food, the price index for eggs increased by 2.0%, pushing the CPI up by about 0.01 percentage points; that for meat of livestock increased by 1.4%, pushing the CPI up by about 0.05 percentage points, of which the price index for pork increased by 1.2%; that for fresh fruits increased by 0.6%, pushing the CPI up by about 0.01 percentage points; that for fresh vegetables increased by 0.5%, pushing the CPI up by about 0.01 percentage points; that for aquatic products decreased by 0.8%, bringing the CPI down by about 0.02 percentage points.

 

Among the price indexes for the other seven major categories, three increased, one remained flat and three decreased month on month. Specifically, the price indexes for transportation and telecommunication, clothing, health care and medical services increased by 1.0%, 0.6% and 0.2%, respectively; the price index for residence remained flat; the price indexes for miscellaneous goods and services, education, culture and recreation, and household facilities, articles and services decreased by 0.8%, 0.3% and 0.1%, respectively.
