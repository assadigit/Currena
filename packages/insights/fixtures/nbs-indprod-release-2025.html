In September, the total value added of industrial enterprises above the designated size increased by 6.5 percent year on year in real terms (the growth rates of value added are the real growth rates after deducting price factors). On a month-on-month basis, in September, the total value added of industrial enterprises above the designated size increased by 0.64 percent over the previous month. From January to September, the total value added of industrial enterprises above the designated size increased by 6.2 percent year on year.

 

 

In terms of three sectors, in September, the value added of the mining industry increased by 6.4 percent year on year, that of the manufacturing industry increased by 7.3 percent, and that of the production and supply of electricity, thermal power, gas and water increased by 0.6 percent.

 

In terms of economic types, in September, the value added of state-holding enterprises increased by 6.5 percent year on year; that of share-holding enterprises increased by 6.8 percent; that of enterprises funded by foreign investors and investors from Hong Kong, Macao and Taiwan increased by 5.8 percent; and that of private enterprises increased by 4.6 percent.

 

In terms of industries, in September, the value added of 36 of the 41 major industries continued to show year-on-year growth. Among them, the value added of the mining and washing of coal went up by 6.4 percent, that of the extraction of petroleum and natural gas up by 8.9 percent, that of the processing of food from agricultural and sideline products up by 6.0 percent, that of the manufacture of liquor, beverages and refined tea up by 0.8 percent, that of the textile industry up by 2.2 percent, that of the manufacture of raw chemical materials and chemical products up by 9.0 percent, that of the manufacture of non-metallic mineral products up by 0.2 percent, that of the smelting and pressing of ferrous metals up by 2.2 percent, that of the smelting and pressing of non-ferrous metals up by 8.4 percent, that of the manufacture of general-purpose machinery up by 9.3 percent, that of the manufacture of special-purpose machinery up by 5.4 percent, that of the manufacture of motor vehicles up by 16.0 percent, that of the manufacture of railway, ship, aerospace and other transport equipment up by 10.3 percent, that of the manufacture of electrical machinery and apparatus up by 7.9 percent, that of the manufacture of computers, communication equipment and other electronic equipment up by 11.3 percent, and that of the production and supply of electricity and thermal power up by 0.5 percent.

 

In terms of products, in September, the output of 362 out of 623 products produced by industrial enterprises above the designated size increased year on year. Among them, the output of rolled steel was 124.21 million tons, up by 5.1 percent year on year; that of cement was 154.44 million tons, down by 8.6 percent; that of ten kinds of non-ferrous metals was 6.95 million tons, up by 2.9 percent; that of ethylene was 2.99 million tons, up by 5.3 percent; that of motor vehicles was 3.227 million sets, up by 13.7 percent, including 1.58 million new energy vehicles, up by 20.3 percent; the electricity generation was 826.2 billion kWh, up by 1.5 percent; and the crude oil processing capacity was 62.69 million tons, up by 6.8 percent.

 

In September, the product sales ratio of industrial enterprises above the designated size was 96.7 percent, a year-on-year increase of 0.6 percentage points; and the export delivery value realized by industrial enterprises above the designated size stood at 1,476 billion yuan, a nominal increase of 3.8 percent year on year.

Annotations:

 

1. Explanatory Notes

 

Growth rate of total value added of the industrial enterprises: also known as industrial growth rate, which is used to reflect a certain period of increase or decrease in volume of industrial production indicators of the degree of change. The indicator can be used to estimate the short-term trend of the industrial economy, and to judge the extent of the economic expansion. It is also an important reference and basis for the formulation and adjustment of economic policies, and for the implementation of macroeconomic adjustment and control.

 

Sales ratio: refers to a ratio of sales value to total industrial output value, which reflects the situation between production and sales of industrial products.

 

Export delivery value: refers to the value of products exported by industrial enterprises on a self-operated or consignment basis (including those sold to Hong Kong, Macao and Taiwan) or handed over to foreign trade departments for export, and the value of products produced by foreign companies such as samples, materials processing, parts assembly and compensation trade.

 

Average daily product output: the total output released by the industrial enterprises above the designated size in the current month divided by the number of days in the month.

 

2. Statistical Coverage

 

The statistical coverage of industrial enterprises above the designated size includes industrial enterprises with annual main business revenue of 20 million yuan or above.

 

As the coverage of industrial enterprises above the designated size changes every year, to ensure the comparability between the data of the current year and those of the previous year, when calculating the year-on-year growth rates of product output and other indicators, data of the previous year have been adjusted according to the statistical coverage of the current year, and thus vary with those released in the previous year in statistical coverage. The main reasons are: (1) Coverage of statistical units changes. Each year, some enterprises are included in the survey once they reach the requisite size, while others exit the survey as their scale decreases. The coverage is also affected by newly established enterprises, bankruptcies and enterprises whose business licenses have been canceled (revoked). (2) There is cross-regional repeated calculation in the product output data of some enterprise groups (companies), which has been eliminated according to specific surveys.

 

3. Survey Methods

 

The reports of industrial enterprises above the designated size are conducted with a comprehensive survey on a monthly basis (excluding the report for January).

 

4. Industrial Classification Standard

 

The New Standard on Industrial Classification for National Economic Activities (GB/T 4754-2017) is implemented.
