6 · The RangeBand family — position in its own decade (§11.6.1, §12.1.1)

The canonical mark for level/index/ratio/percent readings. Track = 10y min–max with end labels; soft zone = 20th–80th percentile; dot = today, hued by impact-through-polarity (never by position); band word rides as a label; the basis disclosure is mandatory (technical tier full, expanded simplified). Magnitude axes stay LTR in both locales (the analytical-axis rule); the fa variant localizes labels and digits only.

7 · CFTC positioning — the two-track discipline (§12.4.5)

Net (−100..+100) and gross (0–100) NEVER share a geometry without labels. Net: centre-zero tick, fill grows from the centre, UNCLAMPED by design (an anomaly renders past the end — a clamp would lie). Gross: origin-anchored share, complement neutral. Definitions verbatim from the records at the technical tier. Canonical home: the Gold Positions section (F-05 dedup: the seats + context cards anchor here).

8 · Yield decomposition — the StackedComposition (§12.4.2)

An additive identity only: nominal = real + breakeven, same-asOf printed, totals reconcile by construction (a mismatched set shows the honest "cannot decompose" state). Components wear NEUTRAL inks — the decomposition is arithmetic, not votes; impact lives on the three records' own cards. The 30-day line names which piece moved.

11 · The relationship explorer — class grammar (§12.5)

Radial hub on desktop (gold at center, the five lane clusters + market-structure nodes around), list-first on mobile. Class is ALWAYS visible: M solid · C dashed · X solid + flag pennant · K heaviest + evidence tag. Blocked edges (ETF flows) live in the legend only. The honesty footer carries the meter's own no-edge record — the map is conventional thinking, never a proven system.

M mechanism (R-01, R-02, R-05…) C correlation (R-19, R-20…) X conditional — pennant, never fine print (R-01's 2022 break, R-22…) K causal-with-evidence — names its evidence (R-09 post-2022 shift) blocked — legend only (R-26 ETF flows: explained conceptually, no data under licence)
Honesty footer (standing): the 37-voter meter's own measured record — no directional edge; sign-agreements 46.8 / 42.2 / 32.7% at 5 / 20 / 60 trading days. This map explains the conventional thinking the meter encodes, not a proven system.
