## 6. Method and limitations

Celle = S2 audit (2026-11-17 ~02:10–03:20 UTC) + this session's live
spot-check (~03:30 UTC: GPE-S2-01, -09, licence wall, honest record,
COT Nov-3 stale — all re-confirmed; no other Currena re-audit, per
the S3 prompt). Selle = S3 audit (same day ~03:02–03:40 UTC, the
companion doc). The 12-dimension scores are the audits' own §4 tables;
the winner column is a dimension-level judgment on those scores +
citations. The raw sum (41.5 vs 39.5) is unweighted and is *one* lens
— §5's qualitative verdict is the governing one; weighting the
dimensions by the program's strategy (explanation-layer priority)
would change the sum's meaning, not the verdict. Both pages were
compared on the same day at the same market conditions; live values
move. No GPE code or shared bookkeeping was touched (standing
constraint).
