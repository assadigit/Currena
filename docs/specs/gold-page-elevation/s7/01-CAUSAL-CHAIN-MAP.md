# S7/01 — The Causal-Chain Map: the 8 Chains Decided on the S6 Zones (M1)

**Session:** S7 of the Gold Page Elevation program (Phase B, session 2 of 2
before CP-B) · **Date:** 2026-11-17 · **Branch:** `docs/gold-page-elevation`

**What this is:** the page-level representation decision for each of the 8
causal chains (`s1/03` — mechanism, indicators, exceptions, horizon, visual
treatment; chain numbers are the stable identifiers). For every chain this
spec decides: **which S6 zone renders it, in what visual form, and how its
honest exception is treated** — plus the data dependencies and the binding
copy obligations (the `s6/02` rules C1–C7, cited per chain). The two
mandated designs — the **Zone K Chain-1/Chain-4 companion chart** (the 2022
crossover, pre-registered windows) and the **Zone F interpretation
paragraph** (the Chain 2+8 teaching) — are spec'd here in full. S8 sequences
what lands in which wave; this doc is the content design of record.

**Method.** Each chain was walked against the 16-zone map (`s6/01 §1`), the
density mechanics (`s6/01 §4`), the module map (`s6/03 §2`), the copy rules
(`s6/02` C1–C7), and the S6-D8 CP-A defaults (scope = the S5-D1 tiers,
F1–F27; Treasury-direct adopted into S8; the two build-time reads pending).
Nothing here adds a zone, a feature, or a data family outside the locked
scope; where a chain's natural visual exceeds the locked scope, that is
recorded as a deferred option for S8, not a commitment. Nothing was
implemented (standing constraint).

## 0. The governing rules this map obeys

1. **Every chain gets a decided representation** — including the decision
   *not* to visualize one. "Prose + cross-links" is a legitimate form; a
   chart that adds no information beyond its cards is the anti-pattern
   (Chain 7's finding below).
2. **The honest exception renders inline, at the teaching, every time** —
   a mechanism taught without its break is the half-truth class the audits
   penalized (GPE-S3-04: a number whose convention is undisclosed; the
   generalized rule: *a mechanism whose exception is unstated*). The three
   mandated exception treatments — Chain 1's 2022 break, Chain 3's
   term-premium twist, Chain 6's fuel-not-driver framing — are binding copy
   clauses below, not optional footnotes.
3. **The pre-registered-window rule** (`s6/02` C1, from GPE-S3-05 and
   Idea 3's risk note): every statistic that depends on a window or a
   sampling choice states it **on its face**, and the window is registered
   in this spec set — never chosen after looking at the data. The registry
   lives in `s7/03 §4`.
4. **The do-not-copy list** (matrix §5) binds every visual: no mixed
   spot/futures conventions (C1), no month-only short-range axis labels, no
   prior-close values unlabeled beside live ones, no commerce funnels, no
   ungoverned template prose, no mixed timezones (name the zone at point of
   use).
5. **The meter is never re-weighted by a chart.** A chain visual teaches;
   the 37-seat meter votes; the two planes stay separate (the S6-D2
   display-only rule for Zone K, the same rule for the TA band — C5 rule
   4). No visual in this spec adds a voter.

## 3. The mandated Zone K design — the Chain-1/Chain-4 companion chart (F11)

**What it is (s1/12 §3's own sketch, now decided):** a single panel closing
Zone K, teaching the decade's most important macro lesson — *the old
inverse rule broke when the buyer rotated*.

**The panel's four layers:**

1. **The dual-axis chart.** Gold (XAUUSD daily closes, EODHD) on the left
   axis; the 10-year real yield (TIPS, DFII10/Treasury-direct
   **[default]**) on the right axis, **not inverted** — plain labelled
   axes; the reader sees the pre-2022 mirror and the post-2022 divergence
   with their own eyes. Display window: trailing 10 years (the page's
   decile grammar horizon; registered in `s7/03 §4`).
2. **The rolling-correlation strip** below the chart: 52-week rolling
   Pearson correlation of Friday-close *levels*, rendered over the same
   10-year display window, with the strip's own zero line. Pre-registered
   (the registry entry): *Friday weekly closes, 52-week window, levels,
   trailing 10y display*. The strip is the honest star: it shows the
   relationship *changing over time* — the exact lesson the competitor's
   single static −0.13 number cannot carry (Idea 3's finding).
3. **The 2022 annotation** — a dated marker (2022-02, the reserve freeze)
   on both the chart and the strip, labelled «چرخش خریدار ۲۰۲۲» / "2022:
   the buyer rotated", linking to (a) the F6 episode record and (b) the
   Zone K tracker above it.
4. **The methodology footer on the panel's face** (C1): series named,
   window + sampling printed verbatim ("weekly Friday closes · 52-week
   rolling correlation · trailing 10 years · gold = XAUUSD daily closes
   (EODHD) · real yield = 10-year TIPS (FRED/Treasury)"), plus the
   plain-language correlation caveat — the wording pattern borrowed from
   the competitor's one good sentence (matrix §4b: «یک عامل است، نه فرمول
   پیش‌بینی» / "one factor, not a forecast formula") — and the honest
   note that a different sampling day moves the number (the GPE-S3-05
   lesson stated *as* the reason the windows are pre-registered).

**Why dual-axis + strip, and not a scatter** (the weighed record): a
correlation scatter compresses time out of the relationship — exactly the
dimension that carries this chain's lesson (the correlation *changed*).
The dual-axis preserves the time structure; the strip quantifies it; the
annotation dates it. The scatter was considered (M1's option list) and
rejected for this chain. Dual-axis charts carry their own risk (two
invitingscales invite spurious mirroring) — mitigated by: fixed axis
assignments (gold always left), the strip carrying the quantitative claim
(the axes never argue causation), and C1's face-disclosure.

**Honest bounds.** The panel never states a "current correlation"
headline number detached from its window; the strip is the number. The
panel renders fail-closed when either series is stale (the universal
rider, C6).
