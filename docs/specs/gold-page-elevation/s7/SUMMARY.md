## Key findings in one paragraph

The framework design needed **zero new zones and zero new data families**
— every one of the 8 chains lands inside an S6 container on already-
adopted data paths, and the two S7 inventions are *grammars*, not
structures: the three-move driver paragraph (mechanism → honest limits →
the live close) and the four-move bridge paragraph, both binding. The
page's honesty rules generalized cleanly from the audit findings into a
single education principle — **a mechanism taught without its exception
is the same defect class as a number without its convention**
(GPE-S3-04's lesson, extended from series-naming to chain-teaching: every
one of the 8 chains carries its break inline, mandatorily). The
**five-driver taxonomy was the sharpest decision**: Kitco's five (the
market's lingua franca) beat the lanes-as-five alternative precisely
*because* the two cuts don't align — the misalignments (positioning is
fuel not a driver; central banks are presented not voted; growth is
excluded for measured instability) are the meter's governance made
visible, and the on-ramp now teaches them as its two closing notes
instead of hiding them. And the integration problem dissolved into two
procedural rules — **one projection, byte-identical counts** (the hero,
the narrative, the on-ramp closes, and the seats render the same
numbers, pinned by test — nothing to reconcile because there is only one
truth) and **out-bound-only references** (education links to data;
episodes link to today; data cards never link back) — plus the 2016+
meter-era boundary that keeps the Episode Explorer honest about the fact
that the meter did not exist before its own archive does. The cheapest
finding: three known defects convert into education at zero cost — the
COT one-liner teaches Chain 6's mid-range lesson, FAQ Q6 teaches the
why-lines, FAQ Q5 teaches the حباب divergence.
