# The WGC authorisation request — the DRAFT for the owner's review + send (decision (f), round 2, 2026-11-07)

**What this file is:** the authorisation-request email the owner's
recorded answer (f) asked GF-S11 to draft. The WGC's own terms (read
live 2026-11-06, `gold.org/terms-and-conditions`) require **prior
written authorisation** for any scraping or redistribution — "you are
not permitted to modify, copy, scrape, distribute … without the prior
written authorisation of WGC" — and their CME-data clause forbids
redistribution of CME-sourced figures without CME's written permission;
the GoldHub downloads are registration-gated. This draft asks for
exactly that authorisation, for exactly the cards the gold meter wants,
under commitments the site already keeps. **The owner reviews, edits the
greeting/contact as they like, and sends it from their own mailbox to
`info@gold.org` (the terms' own contact address). Until a recorded
reply, the WGC-sourced family stays context-blocked and NO WGC fetch is
wired** (the standing discipline: never fetch what the terms forbid).

**To:** info@gold.org
**From:** [the owner's mailbox — Ali Assadi]
**Subject:** Permission request: displaying WGC gold statistics with attribution on currena.ir (non-commercial informational site)

Dear World Gold Council team,

I run **currena.ir** — a free, non-commercial, informational website
built for a general audience in Iran and beyond. The site is a currency
converter plus an educational "gold meter": a bilingual (English /
Persian) dashboard that presents gold-relevant market and economic
*conditions* as small, clearly-sourced context cards (central-bank
balance sheets, real interest rates, exchange rates, official-sector
gold statistics, and the like). The site carries no advertising, sells
nothing, offers no trading or account services, and makes no
forecasting claims — it simply shows what the data says, with every
figure attributed to its publisher and every derived calculation
stated openly on the card.

I am writing because your Terms and Conditions require prior written
authorisation for the use and distribution of World Gold Council
material, and I would like to request that authorisation before
displaying any of it.

**What I am asking to display** — a small set of statistics published
by the World Gold Council (principally the *Gold Demand Trends* data
and the monthly ETF statistics), each shown as a single summary figure
with its reference period, on one card per series:

1. **Gold-backed ETF holdings and monthly flows** (the global ETF
   series you publish monthly);
2. **Central bank net gold purchases** (the official-sector series,
   quarterly);
3. **Total gold demand and supply** (the annual Gold Demand Trends
   frame);
4. **Retail bar and coin demand** (consumer demand, quarterly);
5. **Mine production**;
6. **Recycled gold**;
7. **Jewellery and technology fabrication demand**;
8. **Producer hedging**.

**What I commit to**, should you grant permission:

- **Clear attribution on every card** — "Source: World Gold Council"
  with a link to gold.org (and to the relevant Gold Demand Trends
  publication), plus a full source listing on the site's data-sources
  page;
- **No modification of your data** — figures are displayed exactly as
  published, in their original units and reference periods; where the
  site derives anything from them (for example a percent change), the
  derivation is stated explicitly beside the figure;
- **No bulk redistribution** — the site displays summary figures only;
  it does not mirror, re-host, or offer for download your datasets,
  charts, or reports, and it links to your publications rather than
  copying them;
- **Non-commercial use only** — the site is free, ad-free, and carries
  no sponsorship;
- **Respect for your conditions and prompt takedown** — if you grant
  permission subject to conditions, or later ask me to stop displaying
  any series, I will comply immediately.

I have read that some figures you publish incorporate data licensed
from CME Group; if any part of my request would require separate CME
permission, please treat this as my request for your guidance on that
too, or exclude those series — the list above can be reduced to
whatever you are comfortable with.

Thank you very much for your time and for the work the Council does.
I would be glad to provide any further detail about the site, its
audience, or exactly how each figure would appear.

With kind regards,

**Ali Assadi**
Currena — currena.ir
[the owner's reply address]

H4 (producer hedging)** — nine series, one card each, all currently
'awaiting data' with the licence verdicts recorded in the register.
